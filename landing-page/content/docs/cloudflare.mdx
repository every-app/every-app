---
title: Set up Cloudflare
description: Create a Cloudflare account and turn on Workers Paid. About 5 minutes.
---

EveryApp runs in your own Cloudflare account, and Cloudflare bills you directly. Already have an account? Use it.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create an account

Sign up at [dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up), then verify your email. You need a verified email to buy a domain.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn on Workers Paid

Open [Workers plans](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/plans), choose the **Paid** plan ($5 a month) and enter your card. On the free plan, Cloudflare pauses your databases for the rest of the day once they hit a daily limit, and every app stops with them.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What EveryApp adds to your account

The Gateway adds one Worker, one D1 database and one KV store, all named `every-app-gateway`, plus a DNS record for its address. Each app adds its own Worker (named `every-...`), its databases and its address. Cloudflare Access and email add only what you turn on.

EveryApp never touches your other Workers, DNS records or websites.
