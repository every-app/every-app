> **Web analytics on your own Cloudflare account. No cookies.**
>
> Add a 3 KB tag to a site and see its pages, referrers, campaigns, funnels and goals, with a live view. It stores no IP addresses; Cloudflare resolves location at the edge.

You get dashboards for pages, referrers, campaigns, sessions, funnels, goals, retention and devices, plus a live view, read-only share links, and scheduled or threshold email reports. Bots are filtered out.

## Install

```bash
everyapp install https://github.com/every-app/open-analytics
everyapp configure analytics
```

`configure` asks for one key:

- `MAIN_SECRET` (required): 32 or more random characters, for example from `openssl rand -base64 32`. Don't change it later, or visitor counts split.

Email reports also need the Gateway's email sender ([Email](/docs/agent/email)). Without it, reports stay in the app.

Run the install command again to update.

## Add it to your site

Open `https://analytics.<your gateway domain>`, create a site, and paste its snippet into your website:

```html
<script
  defer
  src="https://analytics.example.com/script.js?siteId=YOUR_SITE_ID"
></script>
```

The tracker, the collector and share links work without sign-in. Everything else needs a member of your workspace, and every member can open the dashboards.

## Use it from your agent

[Connect your agent](/docs/connect-ai) to your Gateway, then ask things like "Which referrers grew this week?" or "Add a site for example.com."
