You don't need this page to use Every App. It's here if you're curious.

## Why a Gateway

Every app needs a login, a list of users and rules about who can open what. Building that into each app is slow, and easy to get wrong.

So Every App does it once, in the Gateway. Every request goes through it first. Your apps just get told who the user is.

## Why Cloudflare

Cloudflare runs small programs, called Workers, on its servers around the world. The Gateway is one Worker. The todo app is another.

Cloudflare also gives each Worker a web address and HTTPS on your domain, and a database to go with it. That's everything an app needs, in one account you own.

## Why Workers Paid

The free plan can't run Every App:

- When a free account hits its daily database limit, the database stops answering until the next day. The Gateway reads its database on every request, so every app would go down.
- Free requests get very little computing time. Checking a password safely can take longer.
- The free plan allows only 10 databases, and each app has its own.

## How your app knows who you are

Say you open `todo.apps.yourdomain.com`:

1. The Gateway checks you're signed in and allowed to open the todo app.
2. It attaches a short-lived signed note saying who you are. It's valid for about two minutes, and only for that one app.
3. It hands the request to the todo app over a private connection inside Cloudflare.
4. The todo app checks the note's signature and runs as you.

The todo app has no public address. It can't be reached from the internet at all, only through the Gateway. A request without a valid note is turned away before any of the app's code runs.

## Why one database per app

Each app gets its own database. One app can't read or break another app's data. And deleting an app never touches the others.

## Who can deploy

Anyone who can log in to your Cloudflare account can deploy apps and change the Gateway. Only give that access to people you fully trust.

## What's missing today

- Deploying from the Gateway's web pages. You deploy from the command line for now.
- An activity log of who did what.
- Sending email from your apps.
- Removing someone takes up to two minutes to reach every app.
- The Gateway can't move to a new address once it's installed.
