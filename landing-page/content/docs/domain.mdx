title: Get a domain
description: EveryApp needs a domain on Cloudflare. Buy one there, or bring one you already own.
---

Your Gateway and apps live under one address on your domain, like `apps.yourcompany.com`. If you'd rather leave your main domain alone, buy a new one just for your apps.

## Buy a new domain

The quickest way. It's ready in under a minute.

1. Open [Register domains](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/registrar/register) in the Cloudflare dashboard.
2. Search for a name and select **Purchase** next to the one you want.
3. Fill in your contact and payment details, then select **Complete purchase**.

A `.dev` or `.app` domain costs $1 for the first year, then $12.20 (`.dev`) or $14.20 (`.app`) a year.

## Use a domain you already own

If you bought it elsewhere, like GoDaddy or Namecheap, keep it there and point it at Cloudflare.

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, open [Domains](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/domains/overview) and select **Onboard a domain**.
2. Enter your domain without `www` (for example `yourcompany.com`) and continue.
3. Choose the **Free** plan.
4. Cloudflare copies your existing DNS records. Check that your website and email records are in the list, then continue.
5. Cloudflare shows you two nameservers. Where you bought the domain, replace its nameservers with Cloudflare's two.
6. Wait for Cloudflare's email saying your domain is active. Usually under an hour, at most a day.

<Callout type="warn">
  If you use email on this domain (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or similar),
  make sure its records were copied in step 4 before you change the nameservers.
  Otherwise your email stops arriving.
</Callout>

## Choosing your address

Setup suggests `apps.yourcompany.com`, but any name under your domain works. Apps go one level below it, like `todo.apps.yourcompany.com`. EveryApp creates the DNS records and certificates itself. If something already uses the address, setup stops rather than replace it.

Pick carefully: the Gateway can't move later. To start again at another address, [remove everything](/docs/update#remove-everything) first.
