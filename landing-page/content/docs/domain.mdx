title: Get a domain

Your Gateway and apps live under one address on your domain, like `apps.yourcompany.com`.

Don't want to touch the domain your website and email run on? [Buy a new domain](#buy-a-new-domain) just for your apps; it takes a minute. Using a domain you already own means changing its nameservers where you bought it, so that Cloudflare serves its DNS. Your website and email keep working when step 4 below copies their records first.

## Buy a new domain

The quickest way. It's ready in under a minute.

1. Open [Register domains](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/registrar/register) in the Cloudflare dashboard.
2. Search for a name and select **Purchase** next to the one you want.
3. Fill in your contact and payment details, then select **Complete purchase**.

With Workers Paid, a `.dev` or `.app` domain costs $1 for the first year. After that it renews at $12.20 a year for `.dev` and $14.20 for `.app`.

## Use a domain you already own

If you bought it elsewhere, like GoDaddy or Namecheap, keep it there and point it at Cloudflare.

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, open [Domains](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/domains/overview) and select **Onboard a domain**.
2. Enter your domain without `www` (for example `yourcompany.com`) and continue.
3. Choose the **Free** plan.
4. Cloudflare copies your existing DNS records. Check that your website and email records are in the list, then continue.
5. Cloudflare shows you two nameservers. Sign in where you bought the domain, find its nameserver settings, and replace the nameservers there with Cloudflare's two.
6. Wait for Cloudflare's email saying your domain is active. This usually takes under an hour, and at most a day.

<Callout type="warn">
  If you use email on this domain (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or similar),
  make sure its records were copied in step 4 before you change the nameservers.
  Otherwise your email stops arriving.
</Callout>

## You don't touch DNS

Every App creates the DNS records and HTTPS certificates it needs. A new address usually starts working within five minutes, up to ten.

If something already uses the address, setup stops instead of replacing it. Delete that record in Cloudflare (your domain, then **DNS**) or choose another address.

## Choosing your address

Setup suggests `apps.yourcompany.com`, but any name under your domain works, like `tools.yourcompany.com`. Apps go one level below it: `todo.tools.yourcompany.com`.

Pick carefully. The Gateway can't move once it's set up. To start again at another address, [remove everything](/docs/update#remove-everything) first.
