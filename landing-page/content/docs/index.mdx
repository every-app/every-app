EveryApp puts the web apps you build online, in your own Cloudflare account. Each app gets a web address, a database and a sign-in.

Made for a solo founder or a team of three or four who build their own tools with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. EveryApp is open source and free. You pay Cloudflare.

## The two parts

**The Gateway** is your home base, at an address like `apps.yourcompany.com`. You and the people you invite sign in there and open your apps.

**Apps** are what you build, usually with a coding agent. Each one has its own address under the Gateway, like `todo.apps.yourcompany.com`.

## What you can count on

- **Private by default.** Nobody opens an app until they've signed in and you've given them access. An app can open specific routes to the public, like a webhook, and the deploy lists them.
- **One sign-in for every app.** You never build a login page.
- **An app only gets what you allow.** It learns who is signed in, never their password. Shared keys, like your AI key, stay on the Gateway.
- **Each app keeps its own data.** Removing one app never touches another.
- **Updates keep your data.** A deploy saves a restore point before it changes an app's database. [Undo a database change](/docs/update#undo-a-database-change)
- **Safe to run again.** If setup or a deploy stops halfway, run it again.

Before you invite anyone, turn on Cloudflare Access with `everyapp access on`. It adds a second sign-in in front of everything, free for up to 50 people. [Sign in through Cloudflare Access](/docs/access)

EveryApp is in early development, so expect some rough edges. To update, run `npm install -g everyapp@latest`, then `everyapp update`. [Update your Gateway](/docs/update#update-your-gateway), [For early adopters](/docs/early-adopters)

## Apps on day one

Each is open source and runs behind your Gateway. Run one as it is, or fork it as the starting recipe for your own. Each has a page with what it does, what it needs and how to install it: [Apps](/docs/apps).

- **[OpenSEO](/docs/apps/openseo)**: SEO research you pay for by use, not by the month.
- **[OpenCRM](/docs/apps/crm)**: A CRM that fills itself in.
- **[OpenAnalytics](/docs/apps/analytics)**: Web analytics on your own Cloudflare account. No cookies.
- **[OpenMail](/docs/apps/openmail)**: An inbox where an AI agent drafts your replies and you hit send.

`everyapp install https://github.com/every-app/<repo>` installs one, and `everyapp configure <app>` asks for the keys it needs. Every key an app can ask for, and where to get it, is in [Providers](/docs/providers).

## What it costs

| What                            | Cost                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Cloudflare's Workers Paid plan  | $5 a month, then usage                               |
| A `.com` domain                 | About $10 a year                                     |
| A `.dev` or `.app` domain       | $1 for the first year                                |
| An AI key, for apps that use AI | Billed by use by OpenAI or OpenRouter, or Workers AI |
| A DataForSEO key, for OpenSEO   | Pay as you go, billed by DataForSEO                  |
| EveryApp                        | Free and open source                                 |

The $5 covers 10 million requests a month, far more than a few apps for a small team use. Past that, Cloudflare bills by use ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/)). The Gateway counts what your apps spend on AI and other keys, and can stop at a cap you set. [Usage and caps](/docs/usage)

## What you need

1. A Cloudflare account on Workers Paid. [Set up Cloudflare](/docs/cloudflare)
2. A domain on Cloudflare. [Get a domain](/docs/domain)
3. Node.js, pnpm and git. [Install the tools](/docs/gateway#install-the-tools), or [let your coding agent do it](/docs/gateway#or-let-your-coding-agent-do-it)
4. About 30 minutes. Setting up the Gateway itself is about 15 of them.
