Every App puts the web apps you build online, in your own Cloudflare account. Each app gets a web address, a database and a sign-in.

## The two parts

**The Gateway** is your home base, at an address like `apps.yourcompany.com`. You and the people you invite sign in there and open your apps.

**Apps** are what you build, usually with a coding agent like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. Each one has its own address under the Gateway, like `todo.apps.yourcompany.com`.

## What you can count on

- **Private by default.** Nobody can open an app until they've signed in and you've given them access.
- **One sign-in for every app.** You never build a login page.
- **Each app keeps its own data.** Removing one app never touches another.
- **Updates keep your data.** Before a deploy changes an app's database, it saves a restore point.
- **Safe to run again.** If setup or a deploy stops halfway, run it again. It picks up where it left off.

## What it costs

| What                           | Cost                  |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Cloudflare's Workers Paid plan | $5 a month            |
| A `.dev` or `.app` domain      | $1 for the first year |
| Every App                      | Free and open source  |

A few apps for a family or a small team fit inside the $5. Full details are on Cloudflare's [pricing page](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/).

## What you need

1. A Cloudflare account on Workers Paid. [Set up Cloudflare](/docs/cloudflare)
2. A domain on Cloudflare. [Get a domain](/docs/domain)
3. A Mac, Windows or Linux computer, and about 30 minutes.
