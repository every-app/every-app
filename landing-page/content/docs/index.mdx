Every App puts the web apps you build online, in your own Cloudflare account. Each app gets a web address, a database and a sign-in.

It's for a solo founder or a team of three or four, building the tools their business runs on with a coding agent like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. Every App is open source and free. You pay Cloudflare.

## The two parts

**The Gateway** is your home base, at an address like `apps.yourcompany.com`. You and the people you invite sign in there and open your apps.

**Apps** are what you build, usually with a coding agent. Each one has its own address under the Gateway, like `todo.apps.yourcompany.com`.

## What you can count on

- **Private by default.** Nobody can open an app until they've signed in and you've given them access. An app can declare public routes, like a tracking script or a webhook, and the deploy prints them.
- **An app only gets what you allow.** The Gateway tells it who the person is, for that app only and for about two minutes, plus the app's own secrets you've set. AI and integration keys stay on the Gateway, which makes those calls for the app. It never sees your password or your session.
- **One sign-in for every app.** You never build a login page.
- **Each app keeps its own data.** Removing one app never touches another.
- **Updates keep your data.** Before a deploy changes an app's database, it saves a restore point.
- **Safe to run again.** If setup or a deploy stops halfway, run it again. It picks up where it left off.

Put your Gateway behind Cloudflare Access before you invite people. `pnpm everyapp access on` adds a second sign-in at Cloudflare's edge, with a code to your email or your company's identity provider. It's free for up to 50 people. [Sign in through Cloudflare Access](/docs/access)

The [threat model](https://github.com/every-app/every-app/blob/main/docs/threat-model.md) says what the Gateway promises, to whom, where each promise is enforced, and what's still open.

## Alpha

Every App is alpha: open source, very powerful, moving fast, and trying not to break things. That means:

- A deploy keeps your data and saves a restore point before it changes an app's database. [Undo a database change](/docs/update#undo-a-database-change)
- Updating your Gateway is `git pull`, `pnpm install`, then `pnpm everyapp gateway deploy`. Your people, apps and data stay. [Update your Gateway](/docs/update#update-your-gateway)
- When something must change, the release notes say so.

We don't promise backwards compatibility yet. Expect rough edges.

## Apps on day one

Each runs behind your Gateway.

- **[OpenSEO](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo)**: SEO research, rank tracking, site audits and AI visibility for your sites. Bring your own DataForSEO key for the search data.
- **OpenCRM**: companies, contacts and deals, kept up to date by a research agent.
- **OpenAnalytics**: web analytics for your sites with no cookies, no stored IP addresses and a tracker of about 3 KB gzipped.
- **Agentic Inbox**: a team inbox with an AI agent that receives, reads, drafts and sends email on your domain.

Clone one, run `pnpm install`, then `pnpm everyapp deploy` in its folder. Once a repository publishes a release, `pnpm everyapp deploy <GitHub URL>` installs it; run it again to update.

## What it costs

| What                           | Cost                   |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Cloudflare's Workers Paid plan | $5 a month, then usage |
| A `.com` domain                | About $10 a year       |
| A `.dev` or `.app` domain      | $1 for the first year  |
| Every App                      | Free and open source   |

A few apps for a small team fit inside the $5. Full details are on Cloudflare's [pricing page](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/).

## What you need

1. A Cloudflare account on Workers Paid. [Set up Cloudflare](/docs/cloudflare)
2. A domain on Cloudflare. [Get a domain](/docs/domain)
3. Node.js, pnpm and git. [Install the tools](/docs/gateway#install-the-tools)
4. A Mac, Windows or Linux computer, and about 30 minutes.
