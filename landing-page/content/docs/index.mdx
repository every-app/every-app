EveryApp runs your team's tools in your own Cloudflare account. Install an open source app with one command, or build your own with a coding agent. Everyone signs in once, and the data stays yours.

It's free and open source. You pay Cloudflare, from $5 a month.

## Get set up

The [quick start](/docs/quickstart) takes you from nothing to a working app in about 20 minutes.

## Apps you can install

- [OpenSEO](/docs/apps/openseo): Keyword research, rank tracking and site audits.
- [OpenCRM](/docs/apps/crm): A simple CRM.
- [OpenAnalytics](/docs/apps/analytics): Web analytics without cookies.
- [OpenMail](/docs/apps/openmail): An email inbox with an AI assistant.
- [OpenScribe](/docs/apps/openscribe): AI meeting notes for Mac.

Each one is open source. Run it as it is, or have your agent change it. [All apps](/docs/apps)

## Why it exists

Most business software is rented. You pay per seat each month, your data sits on someone else's servers, and you can't change how it works.

A coding agent can now build or change an app in an afternoon. What it can't give you is a place to run the app, a sign-in, a database and a safe way to share it with your team. EveryApp is that part.

So you own your tools: the apps and their data are in your account, and nothing is sent to us. You can change them, because every app is open source. And you pay for what you use, not per seat.
