---
title: Invite people
description: Invite people to your Gateway and choose which apps each of them can open.
---

Your Gateway is invite-only. Everything below is under **Manage workspace** at the bottom of the Gateway's sidebar. To rename the workspace, select **Rename** there, or run `everyapp workspace rename "Acme Corp"`.

## Invite someone

1. Open the **Users** tab and select **Invite user**.
2. Enter their email and choose a role:
   - **Member** opens the apps they're given.
   - **Admin** can also invite people and manage apps.
3. Select **Invite**.

If your Gateway can't send email, it shows an **Invite link** instead. Send it yourself; it expires after 72 hours. [Set up email](#send-email)

Behind [Cloudflare Access](/docs/access), run `everyapp access on` again after inviting someone, or let your whole domain through once with `everyapp access on --email-domain yourcompany.com`.

## Choose who can open an app

A new app is private to owners and admins. To share it, open the **Apps** tab and the **⋯** menu next to the app.

- **Manage access** picks people one by one.
- **Edit app**, then **Available to everyone**, opens it to every member, including people you invite later.
- The **Groups** tab gives a set of people the same apps at once.

## When someone forgets their password

If your Gateway sends email, they select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. If not, open the **⋯** menu next to them in **Users**, select **Password reset link** and send it to them. It expires after an hour.

## Remove someone

Open the **⋯** menu next to them in **Users** and select **Deactivate user**. They're signed out everywhere. **Reactivate user** undoes it; **Delete user** removes them for good. Only an owner can delete an admin or another owner.

## Owners

A workspace can have several owners. To add one, open the **⋯** menu next to someone and select **Make owner**, or run `everyapp workspace role <email> owner`. The workspace always keeps at least one owner.

## Send email

Optional. With email set up, invites and password resets go out by email, and apps that send mail use the same sender.

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to **Compute & AI** → **Email Service** → **Email Sending**. Select **Onboard Domain**, choose your domain, then **Add records and onboard**.
2. Run:

   ```bash
   everyapp secret set EMAIL_FROM EMAIL_FROM_NAME
   ```

   Enter the address to send from, like `noreply@yourcompany.com`, and the name people see, like `Your Company`.
