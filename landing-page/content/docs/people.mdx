---
title: Invite people
description: Invite people to your Gateway and choose which apps each of them can open.
---

Your Gateway is invite-only: nobody can sign up unless you invite them. Everything below is under **Manage workspace** at the bottom of the Gateway's sidebar.

## Invite someone

1. Open the **Users** tab and select **Invite user**.
2. Enter their email and choose a role:
   - **Member** opens the apps they're given.
   - **Admin** can also invite people and manage apps.
3. Select **Invite**.

If your Gateway can't send email, it shows an **Invite link** instead. Copy it and send it to them yourself. It expires after 72 hours. [Set up email](#send-email)

Behind [Cloudflare Access](/docs/access), run `pnpm everyapp access on` again after inviting someone. Access keeps its own list of who may pass, and only your Cloudflare login can change it, so the Gateway can't add them for you. To stop doing this for people at your company, let your whole domain through once: `pnpm everyapp access on --email-domain yourcompany.com`. [When you invite someone](/docs/access#when-you-invite-someone)

## Choose who can open an app

A new app is private to owners and admins. To share it, open the **Apps** tab and the **⋯** menu next to the app.

- **Manage access** picks people one by one.
- **Edit app**, then **Available to everyone**, opens it to every member, including people you invite later.
- The **Groups** tab gives a set of people the same apps at once.

## When someone forgets their password

If your Gateway sends email, they can select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

If not, open the **⋯** menu next to them in **Users**, select **Password reset link** and send it to them. It expires after an hour.

## Remove someone

Open the **⋯** menu next to them in **Users** and select **Deactivate user**. They're signed out everywhere and can't sign back in. **Reactivate user** undoes it. The owner can also **Delete user**.

## Send email

Optional. With email set up, invites and password resets go out by email. Apps that send mail, like reports or notifications, use the same sender, so setting it up once serves them too.

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to **Compute & AI**, then **Email Service**, then **Email Sending**. Select **Onboard Domain**, choose your domain, then **Add records and onboard**.
2. In your `every-app` folder, run:

   ```bash
   pnpm everyapp secret set EMAIL_FROM EMAIL_FROM_NAME
   ```

   When asked, enter the address to send from, like `noreply@yourcompany.com`, and the name people see, like `Your Company`. The values aren't shown as you type.
