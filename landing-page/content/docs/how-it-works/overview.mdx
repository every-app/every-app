title: How Every App Works
description: A cloud for small software
---

Every App is a cloud for small software: software built for a specific person, team, or workflow. Small software can still be powerful. It might be a focused tool you build with an agent or a complex open-source app you run for your team.

Every App runs in your Cloudflare account. It gives your apps a secure place to run, with hosting, data, login, and permissions managed in one place.

## Set it up once

Deploy Every App to your Cloudflare account and connect a domain you own. Your Gateway becomes the home for your apps at `your-domain.dev`.

You and your teammates sign in to the Gateway to find and open the apps you can use.

## Add apps

Install a supported open-source app or build one with a coding agent. Each app gets its own address, such as `todo.your-domain.dev`, and can have its own database and storage.

Every App handles deployment and connects the app to your Gateway. You can keep adding apps without setting up a new hosting account or login system for each one.

## Share them with your team

Invite teammates once, then choose which apps each person can use. Everyone signs in with the same account across all of your apps.

Your apps remain separate. Giving someone access to one app does not give them access to every app in your Gateway.

## Private by default

The Gateway checks who someone is and whether they can use an app before the request reaches that app. Apps only accept requests that come through the Gateway, so they are not exposed directly to the internet.

You can make specific pages public when an app needs a share link or public endpoint. Everything else stays private.

Read [Security Architecture](/docs/how-it-works/security) for the technical design, or [SDK & App Manifest](/docs/how-it-works/sdk) to see how an app connects to Every App.

Follow the [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) to set up your Gateway, or [build your first app](/docs/build-an-app/create-app) with a coding agent.
