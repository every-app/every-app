---
title: Usage and caps
description: See what your apps spend on AI and data providers, cap them in dollars per month, and get an email when a cap is near.
---

Apps that use AI or a data provider like DataForSEO make those calls through your Gateway, and the Gateway counts them per app. Open **Manage workspace** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Usage** (owners and admins).

## What is counted

- **Provider calls**: every call the Gateway made for an app this month, to AI providers and to services like DataForSEO.
- **Provider cost**: dollars, where the provider reports a price (OpenRouter and DataForSEO do; OpenAI and Workers AI don't).

Your provider still bills you directly; their invoice is the real bill. Calls an app makes with its own key, outside the Gateway, aren't counted.

## Cap an app

In the **Monthly cap** column, select **Set cap** and enter dollars. Or:

```bash
everyapp workspace app cap <app> 20
```

`off` removes a cap. Caps reset each calendar month (UTC). A cap only counts providers that report a price (OpenRouter and DataForSEO), so calls to OpenAI or Workers AI never move it.

When an app reaches a cap, its AI and provider calls stop until next month or until you select **Raise**. The app still opens and shows its data. A burst of calls can run slightly past the cap.

## A default for every app

**Default monthly cap**, above the table, caps every app without its own cap, including apps you add later:

```bash
everyapp workspace cap default 20
```

To exempt one app, select **Edit** in its row and choose **No cap**.

MCP servers added by URL show **Not metered**: each server bills its own way, so they take no cap. Their calls still show here.

## Cap alerts

Once you set a cap, owners and admins get an email at 80% and at the cap (your Gateway needs to [send email](/docs/people#send-email)). Turn it off, or send the same alerts to a webhook, under **Manage workspace → Events**. [Events](/docs/events#email-alerts)

## Audit log

**Manage workspace → Activity → Audit** shows who did what, newest first: invites, installs, caps, connected AI apps, Access changes and more. It's kept for 90 days and never holds a secret. From a terminal, run `everyapp workspace audit`.
