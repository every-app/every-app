Every App is a self-hosted platform for personal web apps. You describe an app to your coding agent, and Every App gives it everything else: hosting, a database, login, and users. Build for yourself, your family, your friends, or your team.

Everything runs on your own Cloudflare account. Hosting starts free, and even with dozens of apps you're unlikely to exceed Cloudflare's $5/month plan.

You don't need to be an engineer. If you can chat with an AI assistant, you can set this up: the [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) includes prompts you paste into your agent so it does the work for you.

## The Gateway

The Gateway is the front door to all of your apps, and it is the only door.

_The Gateway (left) showing your self-hosted apps, and a Workout Tracker app on mobile._

![Gateway and Workout Tracker](./_assets/gateway-and-workout-tracker.png)

You log into the Gateway once. Every app you install gets its own web address behind it (like `todo.your-gateway.com`), and every request to an app passes through the Gateway first. The Gateway checks who you are, then hands the app a short-lived signed pass that says "this is Ben." Apps never see your password, never store your session, and never implement login.

This design is what makes it safe to build apps quickly with AI. An app you vibe-coded last night rejects any request that didn't come through the Gateway, so a forgotten auth check can't expose it to the internet.

### What the Gateway gives you

- **One URL** for all your apps, on desktop and mobile.
- **Users.** Invite family or teammates to your Gateway and they can use your apps.
- **Mobile.** Add the Gateway to your home screen once; every app benefits.
