Welcome to the Every App blog! This is where we'll share updates, tutorials, and insights about building self-hosted apps on Cloudflare.

## What is Every App?

Every App is a platform for self hosting apps. It standardizes how to build them on Cloudflare so that your apps scale to zero and also to infinity.
