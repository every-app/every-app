- **Do not merge PRs that touch the gateway's auth, the perimeter, MCP auth or platform policy** without Ben. Docs, CLI, SDK dev tooling and skills auto-merge when green (Ben's rule of 2026-10-23).

- **Do not turn passwords off on everyapp.team** (`EMAIL_PASSWORD_SIGN_IN=false`) before Carter has signed in once through Access.

- **Do not ship Postiz, OpenSEO on everyapp.com, Google, Workers AI or the email providers as launch features.** They are ports and capabilities for later; the capability docs can stay but the launch post doesn't mention them.
